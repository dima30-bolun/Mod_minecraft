# Story Bible

## Emberglass Expanse
Biome: volcanic crystal wasteland. Final boss: Cinder Regent. NPC: emberglass_keeper. Arc levels: 1-10.
## Mossveil Archive
Biome: living library jungle. Final boss: Root-Scribe Myra. NPC: mossveil_keeper. Arc levels: 11-20.
## Tideclock Reefs
Biome: tidal clockwork ocean. Final boss: Margrave of Tides. NPC: tideclock_keeper. Arc levels: 21-30.
## Skyforge Isles
Biome: floating anvil islands. Final boss: Aurum Colossus. NPC: skyforge_keeper. Arc levels: 31-40.
## Umbra Fen
Biome: shadow marsh. Final boss: The Lantern Eater. NPC: umbra_keeper. Arc levels: 41-50.
## Frostloom Steppe
Biome: aurora tundra. Final boss: Needle-Crowned Wyrm. NPC: frostloom_keeper. Arc levels: 51-60.
## Brasshollow Depths
Biome: subterranean brass caverns. Final boss: Gearheart Matriarch. NPC: brasshollow_keeper. Arc levels: 61-70.
## Starfall Observatory
Biome: meteor crater highlands. Final boss: Comet Shepherd. NPC: starfall_keeper. Arc levels: 71-80.
## Mirrordune Mirage
Biome: glass desert of doubles. Final boss: The False Pilgrim. NPC: mirrordune_keeper. Arc levels: 81-90.
## Nexus Core
Biome: fractured hub of all echoes. Final boss: Choir of Severance. NPC: nexus_keeper. Arc levels: 91-100.
